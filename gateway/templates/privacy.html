This Privacy Policy explains how Oblivio Company LLC (the "Provider", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use m-gate (the "Service"). It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

🔒 TL;DR — the short version (the full Policy below still governs)

What we store

  • Your email and a securely hashed password — never plaintext.
  • Your IP (sign-up throttling & abuse prevention) and session cookies.
  • Usage telemetry and the content you create; per-server secrets are encrypted.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use third-party ad or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We don't share data except with infrastructure and the providers you connect.

You can request access, correction, export, or deletion of your data — see §8.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the hosted Service operated by the Provider. It does not apply to third-party services you choose to connect or configure (such as embedding providers or downstream MCP servers), which have their own privacy practices.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a securely hashed password. We do not store your password in plaintext.
  • Technical & security data. Your IP address (used for sign-up throttling and abuse prevention), request metadata, request identifiers, and authentication session and CSRF cookies.
  • Usage & telemetry. Operational records of your activity, such as the methods/tools invoked, status/outcome, latency, timestamps, and aggregate usage counters (e.g., call counts and sandbox-execution seconds) used for quotas, billing metering, analytics, and troubleshooting.
  • Content you provide. Data, code tools, configurations, prompts, and other materials you create or store in your tenant, including documents in your tenant database. Per-server secrets you enter are stored encrypted and are never returned by the admin API after they are written.

3. How we use information

  • to provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Service;
  • to authenticate you and enforce roles, tenant isolation, quotas, and rate limits;
  • to detect, prevent, and investigate abuse, fraud, and security incidents;
  • to monitor performance and improve the Service (including aggregate analytics);
  • to communicate with you about the Service; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies

We use strictly-necessary cookies to operate the admin console: an HttpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in and a CSRF token cookie to protect against cross-site request forgery. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Data storage & security

Data is stored in MongoDB (including MongoDB Atlas), using a physically separate database per tenant for isolation. We apply technical and organizational safeguards such as transport encryption, role-based access control, encryption of stored secrets, and a network-isolated WebAssembly sandbox for code tools. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for safeguarding your credentials and for the data you choose to store.

6. Third-party processors & integrations

  • Hosting / database. We rely on infrastructure and database providers (such as MongoDB Atlas and our hosting provider) to run the Service.
  • Embedding providers. If you or the operator configure an embedding provider (e.g., Ollama, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Voyage AI, or Google Gemini), text you submit for indexing or search may be sent to that provider to generate embeddings, subject to that provider's terms and privacy policy.
  • Downstream servers. When you connect downstream MCP servers or data sources, data may be transmitted to those endpoints at your direction.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, then delete or anonymize it within a reasonable period, unless a longer retention is required by law. Some records expire automatically — for example, sign-up attempt records and certain telemetry are subject to time-to-live (TTL) expiry, and deleted tenants may be soft-deleted for a retention window before the underlying database is permanently removed.

8. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can request these by contacting us (see below); we may need to verify your identity. You can also delete content from your tenant or request account deletion. Because the Service is in beta, some controls may be limited.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18 (or the applicable age of majority). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us so we can delete it.

10. International data transfers

We may process and store information in countries other than the one in which you reside. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we take steps to provide an appropriate level of protection for international transfers.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Effective" date above and, where practicable, provide notice of material changes. Your continued use of the Service after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

12. Contact

m-gate is operated by Oblivio Company LLC. Questions or requests about this Policy or your data? Reach us at oblivio-company.com or on GitHub at github.com/ranfysvalle02.
