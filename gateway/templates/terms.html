2. The Service

The Service is a multi-tenant gateway for the Model Context Protocol (MCP). It provides, among other things:

  • a single authenticated endpoint that aggregates many MCP servers and tools, with per-tenant isolation and role-based access control;
  • semantic and hybrid tool discovery and search powered by MongoDB Atlas (combining full-text, vector, and ranking signals in one query);
  • user-authored "code tools" that execute inside a network-isolated WebAssembly sandbox, with a tenant-scoped database bridge and encrypted per-server secrets;
  • tenant administration, usage metering, quotas, telemetry, and analytics.
