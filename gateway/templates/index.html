Credentials

  

    A credential is what your AI client (Cursor, Claude, VS Code, …) pastes in to
    reach this tenant's tools over MCP. Pick how much it's allowed to do — we mint
    the account, generate a bearer token, and hand you a ready-to-paste config.
    Console operators (the humans who sign in and
    manage this console) are listed separately below.

Embeddings

      
Vector embedding configuration

      

        Choose a scope to configure. The platform default
        applies to every tenant; a tenant override lets one
        tenant bring its own provider and stop following platform changes.
      

    

  


  

    
    
  






  

    
Platform default

    
      Inherited by all tenants
    
  

  

    The platform-wide default embedding provider.
    Every tenant inherits this unless it
    defines its own override on the This tenant
    scope above. Vector width is detected automatically by embedding a probe
    string. Saving a change re-embeds the tool catalog, semantic cache, and
    guardrail signatures across
    all inheriting tenants and rebuilds the
    vector indexes.
